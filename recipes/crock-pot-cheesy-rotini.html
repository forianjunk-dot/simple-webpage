Crock Pot Cheesy Rotini

Back to home

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour 45 minutes
  • Servings: 10

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Half-and-half2 cups
Cheddar cheese soup1 can (10.75 oz)
Butter1/2 cup, melted
Velveeta cheese16 oz package, or half of a 32 oz package
Rotini pasta1 box (16 oz), cooked
Italian bread crumbs1/4 to 1/2 cup, optional

Instructions

  1. Pour the half-and-half, cheddar cheese soup, and melted butter into a 4-quart slow cooker. Stir until the soup is fully mixed in.
  2. Add the Velveeta and cooked rotini. Stir well so everything is coated and combined.
  3. Cover and cook on low for 1.5 to 2.5 hours, until the cheese is melted and the mixture is creamy. Cooking time can vary by slow cooker.
  4. If you like a little crunch, sprinkle Italian bread crumbs over the top of each serving before eating.

Notes

  • This recipe is adapted from Gooseberry Patch’s Slow Cooker to the Rescue.
  • All slow cookers cook differently, so the cooking time may need a little adjustment.
  • Make sure the pasta is already cooked before adding it to the slow cooker.
