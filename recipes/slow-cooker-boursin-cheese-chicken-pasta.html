Slow Cooker Boursin Cheese Chicken Pasta

Back to home

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 30 minutes
  • Cook Time: 4 hours
  • Total Time: 4 hours 30 minutes
  • Servings: 8

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Chicken breasts1 1/2 lb
Black pepper1/2 tsp
Salt1 1/2 tsp
Garlic4 cloves, minced
Italian seasoning2 tsp
Boursin garlic and fine herbs cheese10.4 oz, two 5.2-oz packages
Chicken broth1/2 cup
Orecchiette pasta16 oz, cooked separately
Heavy cream1 cup
Parmesan cheeseFor serving

Instructions

  1. Add the chicken breasts to the slow cooker. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, minced garlic, and Italian seasoning.
  2. Place the Boursin cheeses on top of the chicken and pour in the chicken broth.
  3. Cover and cook on high for 4 hours or low for 6 hours.
  4. Cook the pasta until al dente according to the package directions.
  5. Shred the chicken with two forks in the slow cooker, then stir in the heavy cream.
  6. Add the cooked, drained pasta and stir until coated with the sauce.
  7. Serve topped with Parmesan cheese if desired.

Notes

  • Boneless skinless chicken thighs can be used instead of chicken breasts.
  • Hearty pasta shapes such as penne, rigatoni, cavatappi, or bow ties also work well.
  • Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
