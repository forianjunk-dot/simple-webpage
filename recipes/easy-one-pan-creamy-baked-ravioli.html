Easy One Pan Creamy Baked Ravioli

Back to home

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 1 hour
  • Stand Time: 10 minutes
  • Total Time: 1 hour 20 minutes
  • Servings: 10

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Frozen cheese ravioli1 package, 25 oz
Alfredo sauce3 1/2 cups, store-bought
Rotisory chicken1 bird shreaded
Baby spinach3 cups
Shredded mozzarella cheese2 cups, divided
Freshly grated Parmesan cheese1/2 cup, divided

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Lightly grease a 3-quart baking dish.
  2. Spread about 1/2 cup of Alfredo sauce across the bottom of the dish. Arrange half of the ravioli in a single layer over the sauce.
  3. Top with the chicken, and spinach. Pour 1 1/2 cups of Alfredo sauce over the spinach mixture.
  4. Sprinkle with half of the mozzarella and Parmesan, then top with the remaining ravioli in a single layer.
  5. Pour the remaining sauce over the ravioli and top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Cover and bake for about 1 hour, uncovering during the last 15 minutes, until golden around the edges and bubbly.
  7. Let stand for 10 minutes before serving.

Notes

  • The recipe uses frozen ravioli and prepared Alfredo sauce for convenience.
  • Spread the sauce to the edges of the baking dish to help prevent the ravioli from drying out.
  • Nutrition per serving is approximately 741 calories, 58g fat, 25g carbohydrates, and 31g protein.
