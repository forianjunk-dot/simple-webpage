Bobby Flay Italian Meatballs

Back to home

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Ground Beef (80/20) 1 1/2 Pounds
Ground Pork 1/2 Pound
Italian BreadCrumbs 1 Cup
Milk 1/2 Cup
Parmigiana 1/2 Cup
Chopped Fresh Parsley 1/4 Cup
Chopped Fresh Oregano 1 Tablespoon
Cloves garlic minced 4
Large Eggs 2
Salt 2 teaspoons
black pepper 1/2 teaspoon
olive oil 3 Tablespoons

Instructions

  1. Preheat Oven to 375°F.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the panko breadcrumbs and milk. Let stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the ground beef, ground pork, Parmigiano-Reggiano, parsley, oregano, minced garlic, eggs, kosher salt, and black pepper to the bowl. Gently mix with your hands until just combined, do not overmix.
  4. Form the mixture into 2-inch meatballs, about 20-24 total.
  5. Coat balls in olive oil and place on a baking sheet. Cook for aproxamatly 20 minutes.
