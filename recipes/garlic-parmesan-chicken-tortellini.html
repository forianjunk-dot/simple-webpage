Garlic Parmesan Chicken Tortellini

Back to home

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 40 minutes
  • Servings: 4

Ingredients

IngredientAmount
Cheese tortellini12 oz
Chicken breasts2, cut into bite-sized pieces
Olive oil2 tbsp
Garlic4 cloves, minced
Heavy cream1 cup
Chicken broth1/2 cup
Freshly grated Parmesan cheese1 cup
Broccoli florets2 cups
Italian seasoning1 tsp
Fresh parsleyChopped, for garnish
Salt and black pepperTo taste

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Cook the tortellini according to the package directions, reserving 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken until cooked through, then remove it from the skillet.
  3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the garlic and saute until fragrant, then add the broccoli and cook until bright green and tender-crisp.
  4. Pour in the heavy cream and chicken broth. Simmer for 2-3 minutes, then gradually stir in the Parmesan until smooth and creamy. Add reserved pasta water as needed.
  5. Stir in Italian seasoning and season with salt and pepper to taste. Return cooked chicken to the skillet and stir to coat.
  6. Add the drained tortellini. Toss gently to combine and heat through.
  7. Serve immediately with fresh parsley.

Notes

  • Freshly grated Parmesan melts more smoothly than pre-grated cheese.
  • For a vegetarian version, omit the chicken and use vegetable broth.
  • Frozen tortellini can be used; cook it according to the package directions.
