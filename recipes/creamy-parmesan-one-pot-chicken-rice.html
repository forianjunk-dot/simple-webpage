Creamy Parmesan One Pot Chicken and Rice

Back to home

Recipe Details

  • Prep Time: 5 minutes
  • Cook Time: 25 minutes
  • Total Time: 30 minutes
  • Servings: 4

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Chicken breasts 1.5 lb, cut into 1-inch pieces
Butter 4 tbsp
Onion 1 large, diced
Garlic 3 cloves, minced
Italian seasoning 2 tsp
Black pepper 1/2 tsp
Salt 1 tsp
Chicken broth 2 1/2 cups
Long grain white rice 1 cup
Heavy cream 1/2 cup
Freshly grated Parmesan cheese 1/2 cup
Parsley Optional, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the diced onion and cook for 2-3 minutes, until beginning to soften.
  3. Add the chicken pieces along with the Italian seasoning, pepper, and salt.
  4. Cook and stir for about 5 minutes, until the chicken is golden on all sides.
  5. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute, stirring constantly.
  6. Pour in the chicken broth and add the rice. Stir to combine.
  7. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to medium-low.
  8. Cover with a lid and simmer for 17-20 minutes, until the rice is fully tender and the liquid is absorbed.
  9. Stir in the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese until smooth and creamy.
  10. Serve immediately, topped with parsley if desired.

Notes

  • Use long-grain white rice, not instant rice, for the best texture.
  • For the richest flavor, freshly grate the Parmesan instead of using pre-shredded cheese.
  • This recipe is best served right away while the sauce is creamy and the rice is tender.
