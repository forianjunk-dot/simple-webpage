Low-Carb Firecracker Ground Chicken

Back to home

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Ground chicken, 92/8 1 lb
Chili oil 1/2 tbsp
Swerve brown sugar 1/2 cup
Buffalo sauce 1/4 cup
Cider or rice vinegar 2 tbsp
Ground ginger 1 tsp
Garlic powder 1 tsp
Kosher salt 1/2 tsp
Black pepper 1/2 tsp
Red pepper flakes 1/2 tsp
Scallions, sliced Optional garnish
Toasted sesame seeds Optional garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with the chili oil.
  2. Brown the ground chicken on both sides for about 3-4 minutes per side, then mince and finish cooking.
  3. While the chicken cooks, whisk together the Swerve brown sugar, buffalo sauce, vinegar, ginger, garlic powder, salt, black pepper, and red pepper flakes in a bowl.
  4. Once the chicken is fully cooked, add the sauce to the skillet.
  5. Cook until the sauce thickens and becomes sticky and syrupy, about 2-3 minutes.
  6. Serve over rice or cauliflower rice and top with scallions and toasted sesame seeds if desired.

Notes

  • Each serving has about 185 calories and 22 grams of protein.
  • For a lower-carb option, serve over cauliflower rice.
  • Using 97/3 ground chicken will make it a bit leaner, but 92/8 is a good balance of flavor and texture.
  • For a sweeter version, regular brown sugar can be used in place of Swerve.
